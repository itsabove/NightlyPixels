With the release of *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake*,  FOMO has been substantially greater compared to almost any game that’s been released this year.  Maybe it’s due to the fact that I never actually completed a single *Zelda* game in all my years of playing. Now, not finished isn’t the same thing as not played, and I have given the franchise a try on several occasions. I’ve played many titles, like *Oracle of Seasons*, *Windwaker*, and a few others for GameCube and Game Boy, as well as *Breath of the Wild*. Yet none seem to click for me, which begs the question: do I like this genre? Or is *Zelda* just not for me?

<h1>What makes Zelda special?</h1>
<br>

I’ve asked myself whether this type of game is for me, and maybe it’s not. Looking at the list of *Zelda*-likes, I have to be honest with myself and say I haven’t played many of them. Titles like *Ōkami* show up often, as do other games I have never tried. The only game that shows up that I was a huge fan of is *Death’s Door*. I know 3D modern *Zelda* is different, but it sometimes gets compared to older titles like *A Link to the Past*, *Link's Awakening*, and *A Link Between Worlds*. 

![001](https://nightlypixel.imgix.net/nightlypixel/002.png)

Since *Death’s Door* is the only game I have in common with most of the list, I wonder if I just tried the wrong *Zelda* for me. What made this game special to me was that I enjoyed almost every bit of it. The story was sweet, the gameplay was just right, and it felt really amazing to play. It had a fair share of cool bosses with fun mechanics, and a whole journey that was quite enjoyable. 

I wrote this post to see whether I'm going to try to play this remake, which I probably won’t. I don’t currently have a Switch 2, and I haven’t been compelled to buy one if not for this game. I haven’t been interested in any other Nintendo game that’s exclusive enough to make it worth purchasing. I have plenty of other ways to play previous *Zelda* games; I might try to play some of the older titles instead, maybe it gets rid of the FOMO, or maybe it turns me away from *Zelda* in general. All I can say is that the buzz for this Remake has made me really interested in this franchise again, enough to make me want to give it another shot, and to me, that’s a sign of something magical that this game has.
