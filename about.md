<h1 align="center"><b>About Us</b></h1>

<br>

![001](https://nightlypixel.imgix.net/NR_0072-86.png)

<br>

Being a creative and writing about games isn't always all fun; you want to make revenue in this endless cycle of profiting off everything, even leaving opinion pieces and games you enjoy behind just to chase the "trendy" game. But I want to make an effort to do more than just chase something for the pop. I believe there's space regardless of how big or small it is, and I want to put it in my plans to do something about it.

Here at Nightly Pixel we're putting our interest and passion ahead of anything else. Driven to give these spaces that get overlooked some value. We will be writing about all things gaming with a focus on criticism and opinions.

I have hopes and dreams of building a space for people who still enjoy this form of content to find new games, or just to read opinions on something they like. Today it's just me writing previews, reviews, opinions and more. 

Thanks for being here,

Jose Balderas
